# LIU IT Support Guide

supportArea: IT

Source notes:
- Use this guide for general Lebanese International University IT support answers in the student portal.
- Public LIU pages list general university contact information at info@liu.edu.lb and phone +961-1-706881/2/3/4. Students should use their campus IT office, Student Affairs, or the relevant campus office for exact account and system actions.
- The assistant must not ask students to share passwords, one-time codes, private tokens, card details, or sensitive personal documents in chat. For account-specific problems, recommend creating an IT ticket.

## General IT Help

Q: What can IT support help with?
A: IT support can help with student portal access, login problems, password reset guidance, university email access, Wi-Fi connectivity, online learning platforms, lab computer issues, printer or scanner problems, file access problems, classroom technology, and technical errors in LIU systems.

Q: When should I create an IT ticket?
A: Create an IT ticket when the issue depends on your account, device, campus network, email, portal access, course platform access, lab computer, printer, or a system error that staff must check.

Q: What should an IT support ticket include?
A: Include your LIU ID, full name, campus, device type, operating system, browser or app, exact error message, when the issue started, steps you already tried, and screenshots if they do not expose passwords or private codes.

Q: What should I never send in an IT ticket or chat?
A: Do not send your password, one-time code, private recovery code, full payment card details, or sensitive personal files unless the official office specifically requests them through an approved process.

Q: Can the assistant fix my IT account directly?
A: No. The assistant can explain troubleshooting steps and help write a clear IT ticket, but it cannot reset passwords, unlock accounts, change access rights, or edit university systems.

## Login, Password, and Account Access

Q: I cannot log in to the LIU student portal. What should I do?
A: Check that your LIU ID or username is typed correctly, try again in a supported browser, clear cache or use a private window, confirm your internet connection, and check whether the problem happens on another device. If it continues, create an IT ticket with the error message and screenshot.

Q: I cannot sign in to my LIU account. What should I do?
A: First make sure you are using the correct LIU ID, username, or LIU email and the correct password. Try a private browser window, another updated browser, and another device or network. If the problem remains, create an IT ticket with the exact error message, screenshot, campus, and when the issue started. Do not include your password or one-time code.

Q: I forgot my LIU portal password. What should I do?
A: Use the official password reset or recovery option if available. If you cannot reset it, contact campus IT or create an IT ticket with your LIU ID and contact information. Do not share your old password in the ticket.

Q: Can IT tell me my password?
A: No. IT should not tell you or ask you to send your current password. Use the official password reset process if available, or create an IT ticket so campus IT can verify your identity and guide you through account recovery.

Q: My account says it is locked or disabled. What should I do?
A: Account lock or disabled status requires IT or the relevant campus office to check your account. Create an IT ticket with your LIU ID, the exact message, and when it appeared.

Q: Why does the portal say my credentials are invalid?
A: Invalid credentials can happen because of a mistyped LIU ID, wrong password, inactive account, password change, expired session, or system issue. Try careful re-entry and password recovery first, then create an IT ticket if the problem remains.

Q: I changed my password but still cannot log in. What should I do?
A: Wait a few minutes, close all browser tabs, clear saved old passwords, try a private window, and test another browser. If it still fails, create an IT ticket and mention the time of the password change.

Q: The LIU portal is not opening. What should I do?
A: Check your internet connection, refresh the page, try a private window, clear cache for the portal site, disable browser extensions temporarily, and try another updated browser. If the portal is still not opening or shows an error, create an IT ticket with the portal link, screenshot, browser name, device type, campus, and time of the problem.

## Email and Communication

Q: I cannot access my LIU email. What should I do?
A: Confirm the email address or username, check the password, try a private browser window, and make sure your account is active. If you still cannot access it, create an IT ticket with the error message.

Q: My LIU email password is not working. What should I do?
A: Make sure you are typing the correct LIU email or username and that saved passwords in the browser are not using an old password. Try a private window and the official password recovery option if available. If access still fails, create an IT ticket with the error message and when the problem started. Do not send your password.

Q: I am not receiving LIU emails. What should I check?
A: Check spam or junk folders, storage limits, forwarding rules, inbox filters, and whether your email address is written correctly in LIU systems. If important university messages are missing, create an IT ticket.

Q: Can IT change my personal email or phone number?
A: Contact the relevant campus office or IT support because personal contact changes may require identity verification and student record updates.

Q: I received a suspicious email. What should I do?
A: Do not click links, open attachments, or reply with personal information. Report it to IT or your campus office and include a screenshot or message headers if safe.

## Wi-Fi and Internet

Q: I cannot connect to LIU Wi-Fi. What should I do?
A: Turn Wi-Fi off and on, forget and reconnect to the network, check that the correct campus network is selected, restart the device, and test another location on campus. If it still fails, create an IT ticket with your device type and campus location.

Q: What is the LIU Wi-Fi password?
A: The assistant cannot provide private or campus-specific Wi-Fi passwords. Ask your campus IT office, Student Affairs, or the relevant campus office for the official connection instructions. If your account should have Wi-Fi access but cannot connect, create an IT ticket with your campus, device type, and error message.

Q: LIU Wi-Fi is connected but there is no internet. What should I do?
A: Test another website, disconnect and reconnect, restart the device, disable VPN temporarily, and try another campus area. If many students have the same issue, report the campus, building, floor, and time to IT.

Q: Does IT support personal devices?
A: IT may provide basic guidance for connecting personal devices to university services, but hardware repair, operating system damage, and private software problems may be outside university support.

Q: Why is the internet slow on campus?
A: Slow internet can be caused by weak signal, crowded areas, device issues, VPN, background downloads, or network outage. Report the campus, building, room, time, and device type in an IT ticket.

## Online Courses and Learning Platforms

Q: I cannot access an online course. What should I do?
A: Check that you are registered for the course, confirm you are using the correct platform and account, refresh or try another browser, and contact the instructor or department if the course is missing from your schedule. Create an IT ticket if the course exists but the platform blocks access.

Q: My online learning platform is not working. What should I do?
A: Confirm that you are using the correct platform and account, refresh the page, try another updated browser, clear cache, and test another network. If a specific course or exam is affected, contact the instructor for academic rules and create an IT ticket with the course name, platform, screenshot, and exact error message.

Q: My course does not appear online. Is this an IT issue?
A: It may be an academic registration issue or an IT synchronization issue. If the course is not on your official schedule, contact Registration or your department. If it is on your schedule but missing online, create an IT ticket.

Q: I cannot upload an assignment. What should I check?
A: Check the file type, file size, deadline, internet connection, browser, and whether the platform accepts the upload. Try renaming the file using simple English letters and numbers. If it fails, screenshot the error and contact the instructor or IT.

Q: My assignment upload failed. What should I do?
A: Check the deadline, file size, file type, file name, and internet connection. Rename the file using simple English letters and numbers, try another browser, and avoid uploading from an unstable connection. If the upload still fails, take a screenshot of the error, contact the instructor if the deadline is urgent, and create an IT ticket.

Q: The online exam or quiz is not opening. What should I do?
A: Check the exam time, browser, internet connection, and platform login. Contact the instructor immediately for exam rules, and create an IT ticket with the error screenshot if the platform itself fails.

Q: A video lecture will not play. What should I do?
A: Try another browser, update the browser, disable extensions, check internet speed, clear cache, and test another device. If the issue remains, create an IT ticket with the course, lecture name, and error message.

## Browser, Device, and App Troubleshooting

Q: What browser should I use for LIU systems?
A: Use a modern updated browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari. If one browser fails, try another browser or a private window.

Q: How do I clear browser cache for a portal problem?
A: Open browser settings, find privacy or browsing data, clear cached images/files and cookies for the site, close the browser, and sign in again. Be careful because clearing cookies may sign you out of websites.

Q: Why does the portal keep loading or show a blank page?
A: It may be a cache, browser extension, network, or system issue. Try refresh, private window, another browser, another network, and then create an IT ticket if it continues.

Q: The portal shows an error message. What should I do?
A: Copy the exact error message or take a screenshot, refresh once, try a private window, and test another browser or network. If the error continues, create an IT ticket with the portal page, error message, time, device type, browser, and steps that caused the error.

Q: What should I do if a file will not download?
A: Check pop-up blockers, browser download permissions, internet connection, storage space, and file access rights. Try another browser and include the file name or page in an IT ticket if needed.

Q: What should I do if an uploaded file is too large?
A: Compress the file, export it as PDF if allowed, reduce image resolution, or ask the instructor or office for the accepted file size and format.

## Lab Computers, Printing, and Campus Equipment

Q: A lab computer is not working. What should I do?
A: Report the lab, computer number if visible, campus, time, and the exact problem to campus IT or create an IT ticket. Do not attempt hardware repair yourself.

Q: I cannot print on campus. What should I check?
A: Check printer status, paper, selected printer, print balance or permissions if applicable, file format, and network connection. If it fails, report the printer location and error message.

Q: Classroom projector or audio is not working. What should I do?
A: Report the room number, campus, class time, device used, and problem to campus IT or the relevant office. For urgent class issues, ask the instructor or department to contact campus support.

## Security and Privacy

Q: How can I keep my LIU account safe?
A: Use a strong unique password, do not share it, sign out on shared computers, avoid suspicious links, keep your device updated, and report unusual account activity quickly.

Q: Someone knows my password. What should I do?
A: Change the password immediately using the official process if possible, sign out of other sessions if available, and report the issue to IT. If you cannot access the account, create an urgent IT ticket.

Q: I clicked a suspicious link. What should I do?
A: Change your password if you entered it, scan your device, do not enter more information, and report the incident to IT with details of the message or website.

Q: Can I share my account with a friend?
A: No. Students should not share LIU account credentials. Account sharing can expose private records and may violate university rules.

## Escalation Rules

Q: When should the assistant escalate an IT question?
A: Escalate when the question depends on a student's account, password reset, locked account, email access, Wi-Fi access, course platform access, exam platform issue, device-specific failure, or any problem requiring campus IT action.

Q: What can the assistant safely answer for IT?
A: The assistant can give general troubleshooting steps, help identify what information to collect, explain likely causes, and draft a clear IT ticket.

Q: What should the assistant avoid saying for IT?
A: The assistant should not claim it reset a password, unlocked an account, changed records, fixed Wi-Fi, approved access, guaranteed platform availability, or verified private account status.
