# LIU Admission, Registration, and Student Support Guide

Source notes:
- Based on public Lebanese International University pages and catalog snippets checked on 2026-04-28.
- Use this document for general portal answers. For final decisions, students should contact the relevant LIU campus office, Admission and Registration, or Student Affairs.

Q: What does the LIU Admission and Registration Office do?
A: The Admission and Registration Office handles enrollment applications, admission conditions, registration guidelines, and student records processes connected to LIU and Ministry of Education and Higher Education requirements.

Q: Where can students apply to LIU?
A: Students can usually submit an online application through LIU systems or apply directly through an admission office with help from admission staff.

Q: What documents are commonly requested from transfer students?
A: Transfer students are commonly asked for a certified copy of the Lebanese Baccalaureate, Technical Baccalaureate, or recognized equivalent, plus a certified university transcript and course descriptions for transfer evaluation.

Q: Does LIU accept transfer credits?
A: Yes. Transfer credits may be accepted from recognized institutions after review and academic approval by the relevant school or department.

Q: Do accepted transfer credits count in the LIU GPA?
A: Accepted transfer credits generally do not count in the student's LIU GPA.

Q: How many credits must a transfer student complete at LIU?
A: Depending on the school, a transfer student is generally required to complete at least 51% of credits at LIU to be eligible for a degree.

Q: Can a department refuse transfer credit for a major requirement?
A: Yes. The relevant department may decide not to grant transfer credit for major requirement courses, even when similar courses were completed elsewhere, if it needs to ensure the required outcomes are met.

Q: What language are LIU courses delivered in?
A: LIU courses are delivered in English, so admitted students are expected to show an appropriate level of English proficiency.

Q: How can students show English proficiency?
A: Students may show English proficiency through accepted standardized tests or through LIU placement and English course requirements, according to the level assigned by the university.

Q: Is admission always valid in later years?
A: Admission is generally valid for the ongoing academic year for undergraduate and graduate programs. Students who do not register may need to apply again or confirm their status with Admission and Registration.

Q: How are LIU tuition fees commonly calculated?
A: LIU tuition fees are calculated according to the number of credits. Other costs, such as registration fees, social security fees, and books, may be separate from the credit price.

Q: What is GPA?
A: GPA means Grade Point Average. It summarizes a student's academic performance based on course grades. Students should verify their official GPA and exact calculation with Registration, their department, or their academic advisor.

Q: What is the freshman credit requirement?
A: To complete a freshman program, students generally complete at least 30 credits in Freshman Arts, Freshman Sciences, or Freshman Engineering.

Q: What does Student Affairs help with?
A: Student Affairs supports areas such as financial aid guidance, social security and NSSF processes, student activities, counseling, student engagement, educational services, complaints, grievances, and appeals.

Q: What is NSSF clearance?
A: LIU Student Affairs describes the Social Security Unit as a liaison between students and the National Social Security Fund. For Lebanese students who are required to join NSSF, clearance may be required before registration at the beginning of each academic year.

Q: Who may be exempt from NSSF membership?
A: LIU public Student Affairs information says membership requirements exclude freshman and special students and students older than 30. Students with special coverage or exemption questions should contact Student Affairs for the proper process.

Q: Who should a student contact for registration holds or missing documents?
A: The student should contact Student Affairs, Admission and Registration, or the relevant campus office with their LIU ID, program, department, and a clear description of the hold or missing document.

Q: What should the support portal collect before creating a registration ticket?
A: The portal should collect the student's LIU ID, academic department, support area, clear subject, detailed description, and any relevant document or screenshot if available.

Q: When should the assistant escalate a registration question to a ticket?
A: The assistant should escalate when the question depends on a student's personal record, payment status, document approval, NSSF clearance, transfer-credit evaluation, campus-specific decision, or any policy detail not available in trusted knowledge.
